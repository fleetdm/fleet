- Added tvOS (Apple TV) as a supported host platform. Apple TVs assigned in Apple Business Manager now enroll through Automated Device Enrollment, appear as `tvos` hosts in the new "tvOS" built-in label, and have their vitals and installed apps collected by the Apple MDM refetch cron. Apple TVs deleted from Fleet while still MDM-enrolled are re-created when they check in, like iPhones and iPads.
