# NVIDIA GTC Berlin: Fleet brings full NVIDIA AI hardware suite under enterprise IT control

_From [NVIDIA RTX PRO](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/workstations/) workstations to [NVIDIA DGX Station](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/workstations/dgx-station/), Fleet gives IT and security teams one place to deploy, secure and govern NVIDIA-powered devices alongside the rest of the enterprise estate_

**[NVIDIA GTC](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/) BERLIN — Oct. 21, 2026** — [Fleet Device Management](https://fleetdm.com), the open device management platform, today announced support for NVIDIA’s entire AI hardware ecosystem, enabling enterprises to manage [NVIDIA RTX PRO](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/workstations/) workstations and [NVIDIA DGX Station](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/products/workstations/dgx-station/) alongside the macOS, Windows and Linux devices already in their environment. NVIDIA DGX Station is the ultimate deskside AI supercomputer, designed to build and run AI. Featuring the NVIDIA GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra Desktop Superchip and a massive 748 GB of coherent memory, it accelerates large AI development, inferencing, agentic AI, and data science workloads.

The announcement comes as [NVIDIA GTC Berlin](https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/gtc/) showcases the expanding role of AI hardware across the enterprise. NVIDIA RTX PRO workstations and NVIDIA DGX Station systems have introduced data-center-class capabilities into a broader range of professional environments and workflows.

“The AI workstation is now an enterprise endpoint, plain and simple,” said Mike McNeil, CEO and co-founder, Fleet. “IT teams still need to know what’s running, what’s vulnerable and what’s changed. They already have enough on their plates without building a new management stack for every new piece of AI infrastructure. They should be able to manage AI workstations alongside every other device they own, from one place, with the same visibility and controls. That’s what this update gives them.”

“NVIDIA is bringing powerful AI computing directly to developers, creators, engineers and enterprise teams so they can build and run advanced AI workloads locally,” said Chris Marriott, vice president of Enterprise Platforms, NVIDIA. “Fleet’s support for NVIDIA DGX Station and NVIDIA RTX PRO workstations gives organizations a unified way to deploy and manage their AI systems, helping accelerate AI adoption across the enterprise.”

Fleet gives IT and security teams one place to see and manage these systems, including OS, hardware, software, packages, security controls and vulnerabilities. This helps organizations understand where AI compute is operating and whether the underlying devices are secure and compliant.

Fleet enables organizations to manage policies, configurations, deployment waves, reports and remediation through reviewable workflows in Git. Instead of creating one-off processes for new and evolving AI systems, IT teams gain consistent, auditable controls throughout the device lifecycle. This makes changes visible, reviewable and reversible as NVIDIA hardware becomes a larger part of enterprise infrastructure.

To learn more about Fleet’s support for NVIDIA-powered systems, visit [https://fleetdm.com/articles/frontier-hardware-deserves-frontier-device-management](https://fleetdm.com/articles/frontier-hardware-deserves-frontier-device-management).
