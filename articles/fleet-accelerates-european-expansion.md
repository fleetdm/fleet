# Fleet accelerates European expansion as digital sovereignty reshapes enterprise IT

_ISO 27001 certification and GDPR third-party attestation strengthen Fleet's European footprint as Mollie, SUSE and other multinational organizations prioritize control and transparency_

**SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 4, 2026** — [Fleet Device Management](https://fleetdm.com), the AI device management platform for top IT and security teams, today announced an expansion of its European presence, backed by its [ISO 27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) certification and strengthened GDPR third-party attestation as enterprises increasingly prioritize digital sovereignty and control over their device infrastructure.

Organizations want greater visibility into where their technology runs, how data is handled and who controls the infrastructure underpinning critical systems. Fleet provides a single platform for managing and securing macOS, Windows and Linux devices, combining device management, vulnerability management and endpoint visibility. Its ISO 27001 certification provides independent validation of Fleet's information security management practices, while its recently-completed independent GDPR assessment validates that Fleet meets all of the regulation requirements for organizations operating under European data protection requirements.

Fleet's open-source foundation also gives enterprises transparency into the technology managing their endpoints, with the ability to inspect the source code, integrate Fleet into existing infrastructure and choose deployment models aligned with their security and data requirements.

"European regulations are among the most developed globally, and they're asking the right question: who actually controls the technology that runs the enterprise?," said Mike McNeil, CEO and co-founder of Fleet. "Digital sovereignty means being able to see how your infrastructure works, control where your data goes and change direction without asking a vendor for permission and causing unnecessary slow downs. That's what open source has always been about, and it's exactly how we built Fleet."

Among the European organizations using Fleet are [Mollie](https://www.mollie.com/gb/), one of Europe's fastest-growing financial service providers, and [SUSE](https://www.suse.com/), a global leader in enterprise open-source solutions, alongside other large multinational customers operating complex device environments across the region.

"When we evaluate any tool that touches every device in our environment, security posture is non-negotiable. Fleet's ISO 27001 certification, combined with their open-source transparency, means we can see exactly how the product works and trust how our data is protected," said Sam Clark, senior infrastructure manager, Mollie.

Further demonstrating its product security commitment, Fleet recently joined [Project Glasswing](https://www.anthropic.com/glasswing), Anthropic's controlled-access program for Claude Mythos Preview. Fleet is using the program to explore how advanced AI can safely interact with device management systems, MDM protocols and enterprise infrastructure while maintaining human oversight. To learn more, visit the [Fleet Trust Center](https://trust.fleetdm.com).
