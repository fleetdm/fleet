How this is measured

Seats: one per Mac that has the app installed. Only top-level apps in /Applications count. Apple's own apps and apps nested inside other apps are left out.

Idle: not opened in days, or never opened. "Last opened" comes from macOS, through Fleet's software inventory. Apps that only run in the background or start from the command line may show as never opened.

Who's counted: Macs that checked in within the last days. A Mac that's been offline longer would make every app on it look idle.

Groups: 80% or more idle means remove it or unassign it. 40–79% means right-size at renewal. Under 40% is healthy. Only apps on or more Macs are listed. Totals cover every app.

Not seats: agents IT deploys everywhere (Fleet Desktop, Nudge, Santa, Okta Verify) helpers, installers, uninstallers, updaters, and web shortcuts (such as Google Drive's Docs and Sheets launchers). They're listed separately and left out of the totals.

Vendor: taken from the app's bundle identifier, such as com.adobe for Adobe.
